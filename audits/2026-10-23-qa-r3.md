### QA-R3-003: Cash tips are counted as "Card tips owed" and paid out again
- **Area:** dashboard
- **URL:** `/dashboard/platform/commissions`
- **Steps:**
  1. Open Commissions and compare "Card tips owed" with Reports' Recent payments.
- **Expected:**
  - Only tips collected on card are owed to the stylist through payroll.
  - Cash tips are usually handed over at the time. If the salon pools them, they should at least be labelled and filtered separately.
- **Actual:**
  - "Card tips owed $25.00": Maya $20.00 and Leo $5.00.

  - "Pay out" pays the cash tips again with the commission. I paid out Maya, which covered her $5 cash tip.
- **Cause:** checkout writes a `GratuityLedgerEntry` with `payoutStatus: "payable"` for every tip, whatever the payment method. Commissions then sums every `allocation` entry under the "card tips" label.
- **Likely files:**
  - `features/keystone/mutations/completeAppointmentCheckout.ts:322-334` (only card/terminal tips payable, or record the method)
  - `features/platform/commissions/actions/commissions.ts:125`
  - `features/platform/commissions/screens/CommissionOperationsPage.tsx:45,60`
