## Major

### QA-R2-001: Several dashboard pages still print UTC times and dates, so evening visits show on the wrong day
- **Area:** dashboard
- **URL:**
  - `/dashboard/platform/commissions`
  - `/dashboard/platform/reports`
  - `/dashboard/platform/inventory`
  - `/dashboard/platform/integrations`
  - `/dashboard/platform/engagement`
  - `/dashboard/platform/clients/[id]` (first and last visit)
- **Steps:**
  1. Staff-book QA Phone Client R2 for Fri Oct 23, 5:00 PM (salon time zone America/Los_Angeles).
  2. Check out at about 3:43 PM PDT.
  3. Open Commissions, Reports and Inventory.
- **Expected:** "Oct 23, 2026" on the commission line. "Oct 23, 3:43 PM" on the payment and the stock movement.
- **Actual:**
  - The commission line reads "**Oct 24**, 2026 · QA Phone Client R2".
  - Reports' Recent payments shows "Oct 23, **10:43 PM**".
  - Inventory's "Retail sale at appointment checkout" shows "Oct 23, **10:43 PM**".
  - Integrations and engagement events are also 7 hours ahead. For example, the round-1 walk-in added at about 12:19 PDT shows "Oct 23, 7:19 PM".
  - A salon reconciling the day's takings or paying commission by date gets the wrong day.
- **Cause:** Server components call `toLocaleString` or `toLocaleDateString` without `timeZone`, so they render in the server's UTC. This is the same bug as round-1 QA-006, which was fixed only on Schedules and Resources.
- **Likely files:**
  - `features/platform/commissions/screens/CommissionOperationsPage.tsx:18`
  - `features/platform/workspaces/screens/SalonWorkspacePage.tsx:29` (shared by Reports, Inventory, Integrations, Engagement, Catalog and Benefits)
  - `features/platform/clients/screens/ClientProfilePage.tsx:12`
  - All three should use the location timezone, as `features/lib/formatSalonDateTime` already does.
