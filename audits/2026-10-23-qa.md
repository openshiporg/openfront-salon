## Blocker

### QA-001: Dashboard home, the Models menu and every generic list or item route are broken in production
- **Area:** dashboard
- **URL:** `/dashboard`, `/dashboard/services`, `/dashboard/stylists`, `/dashboard/appointments`, `/dashboard/clients`, `/dashboard/users` (and every other `/dashboard/<list>` route)
- **Steps:**
  1. Sign in as admin.
  2. Look at the Dashboard home.
  3. Expand **Models** in the sidebar.
  4. Open `/dashboard/services` directly.
- **Expected:** As in E-commerce, the home page shows "N Models" with cards and counts, the Models menu lists every list, and `/dashboard/services` opens the list table.
- **Actual:**
  - Home shows "No lists configured. There are no data models configured in your admin interface."
  - The **Models** group expands to nothing.
  - Every `/dashboard/<list>` route returns **404**, and the console logs `Dashboard operation failed (details redacted).`
  - Every platform page also fires a server action that returns `{"success":false,"error":"Unknown error"}` on load. This is probably the same failure.
- **Evidence:**
  - With the same admin cookie, POSTing the exact `KsFetchAdminMeta` query from `getAdminMetaAction.ts` to `/api/graphql` from the browser returns 200 with **no errors** (about 413 KB, all lists present).
  - So the failure is only in the **server-side** call: `keystoneClient` → `getGraphQLEndpoint()` → self-fetch of `https://<x-forwarded-host>/api/graphql`.
  - The code is byte-identical to E-commerce, so the cause is probably in the runtime or environment, not the code. The production logs will show the `Error fetching GraphQL data:` summary.
- **Why it's a blocker:** Several day-one records have no platform screen at all: stylist working hours (see QA-009), payment providers, message templates, gift cards, intake forms and roles. The Models section is the only way to manage them, and it's dead.
- **Likely files:**
  - `features/dashboard/actions/getAdminMetaAction.ts`
  - `features/dashboard/lib/keystoneClient.ts` (`redirect: 'error'`, auth headers)
  - `features/dashboard/lib/getBaseUrl.ts`
  - `features/dashboard/screens/HomePage/index.tsx:80`
  - `features/dashboard/actions/getListByPath.ts`

### QA-002: Booking time picker overflows its column: slots and "More dates" are hidden on phones and under the summary on desktop
- **Area:** storefront
- **URL:** `/book`, step 3 "Pick a time"
- **Steps:**
  1. Go to `/book`.
  2. Choose Signature Cut & Blowout, then Maya Chen.
  3. Pick Mon Oct 26.
- **Expected:** The date strip scrolls inside the left column. The time slots wrap inside the column. The "More dates" button is visible.
- **Actual:**
  - **At 390px:** the time panel runs off the right edge of the screen. In the Afternoon row only 1:30, 2:00 and a clipped 2:30 are visible, and 3:00–4:30 PM can't be seen or tapped. The "More dates" button, which is the only way past 14 days, is off-screen.
  - **At 1440px:** the date strip (948px) and the time panel extend under the sticky "Your visit" card. Wed 4 Nov and the "More dates" button sit behind the card. The grid column is 828px but its content is 952px.
- **Cause:** In `TimeSlotPicker`, the root `grid` children keep `min-width:auto`, so the 14-day `.sf-date-strip` (`grid-auto-columns: 4rem`) forces the column wider than the viewport. The `.sf-track-scroll` never gets a constrained width to scroll inside.
- **Likely files:**
  - `features/storefront/components/TimeSlotPicker.tsx:54` (add `min-w-0` or `grid-cols-[minmax(0,1fr)]`)
  - `features/storefront/styles/storefront.css:992` and `:1043`

### QA-003: Inner-page headings render at body size (16px) across the whole storefront
- **Area:** storefront
- **URL:** every page except `/`: `/services`, `/team`, `/gallery`, `/reviews`, `/contact`, `/gift-cards`, `/book`, `/account`, `/account/signin`, `/account/signup`, `/services/[id]`, `/team/[id]`
- **Steps:** Open `/services` at any width and inspect the `h1` "Service menu".
- **Expected:** The display sizes from the tokens: `--sf-text-display` is up to 100px, `--sf-text-3xl` up to 48px, and so on. The home page's `.sf-section-title` renders correctly at 48px.
- **Actual:** Computed `font-size: 16px` on every heading or price that uses `text-[var(--sf-text-*)]`. Examples:
  - "Service menu", "The stylists", "Visit & hours", "Book an appointment" and "Pick a time" (h1/h2)
  - The service name on the service detail page
  - The $85 price
  - "QA Client" and "Appointments" on the account page

  As a result, every page except the home page looks unstyled and has no hierarchy.
- **Cause:** Tailwind treats `text-[var(--x)]` as a **colour** utility, not a font size. There are about 115 such classes in 21 files: `text-[var(--sf-text-sm)]` ×50, `-xs` ×16, `-2xl` ×16, `-display-s` ×9, `-md` ×7, `-xl` ×6, `-lg` ×5, `-3xl` ×5, `-display` ×1. The `-sm`/`-xs` ones also silently render at 16px.
- **Fix:** Use `text-[length:var(--sf-text-…)]`, or CSS classes that set `font-size`.
- **Likely files:**
  - `features/storefront/screens/*.tsx`
  - `features/storefront/components/*.tsx`
  - `features/storefront/styles/storefront-tokens.css:50-57`
